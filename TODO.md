Follow-up items captured by the `code-review` skill when a finding is
marked `later`. Each entry is the agent's verbatim quote from the review
context (file:line + one-line summary), plus a date stamp so the list
stays scannable.

```
- [YYYY-MM-DD] `path/to/file:LINE` — <one-line summary>
```

Items graduate out by being checked off (`- [x]`) when addressed, then
removed in the next history pass.
